# Using the Autofocus

autofocus is a feature that automatically adjusts the camera lens to ensure the subject is in sharp focus. In qShot, the autofocus component is designed to help users capture clear and crisp images or video, even when the subject or camera position changes.

## How Autofocus Works
- The camera analyzes the scene to detect the area with the highest contrast or sharpest edges.
- The lens is adjusted until the selected area is in optimal focus.
- Some cameras support continuous autofocus, which keeps adjusting as the scene changes, while others require manual triggering.

## Using Autofocus in qShot
- Look for the autofocus button or menu option in the user interface.
- When enabled, qShot will attempt to keep the main subject in focus automatically.
- For manual control, you can usually tap or click on the preview to set the focus point, or use a dedicated focus control slider.
- If your camera supports it, you may be able to switch between continuous and single-shot autofocus modes.

## Tips
- Good lighting helps autofocus work more reliably.
- If autofocus struggles, try focusing on an area with more detail or contrast.
- For creative effects, you can disable autofocus and use manual focus controls.
