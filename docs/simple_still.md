# Simple Still

At its most basic the Simple Still component  lets you just 'point and click'. Press the `capture` button and an image is captured and saved. This image will be in jpeg format, at a preset resolution and it will have a name. 

The terminal window will show that the image has been captured and by clicking on the link the image can be previewed. 

Don't like the resolution? Don't like the filename? ant a different image quality? Press the `more` button and a whole range of additional options will be made available.
